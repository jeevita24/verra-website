THE VERRA STORY

Designed to be you.

VERRA is a women's fashion label created around individuality, elegance and effortless confidence.

We believe clothing is more than something you wear. It is part of how you express yourself, how you carry yourself and how you feel in your own skin.

VERRA creates handcrafted, ready-to-wear lehengas designed for moments that deserve something special. Each piece is made with thoughtful details and a focus on elegance, comfort and individuality.

Every VERRA lehenga is made to order, made for you. Before your order is prepared, we speak with you about your size, measurements, blouse preference, skirt length and any customization you may require.

Your order begins with a conversation. Once your requirements are confirmed, we prepare your piece and arrange dispatch.

VERRA is built around the belief that what you wear should feel like an expression of who you are — personal, confident and entirely your own.
